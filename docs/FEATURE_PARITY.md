3. Copytrade: all EVM chains + Solana.

5. Trading fee 1%. On Solana the fee is taken in-transaction; on other chains it accrues per Telegram account and is extracted once above a minimum.
6. Premium: $200 per 30 days (currency unspecified in source). Limits free vs premium: trade monitors 10 vs 30; wallets per chain 8 vs 10; copytrade wallets per chain 5 vs 12; snipes per chain 5 vs 10; limit orders 60 vs 120. Launch Simulator, trending, and Hits are premium-only.
