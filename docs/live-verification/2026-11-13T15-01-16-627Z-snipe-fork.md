## Not covered
- NO pre-trade sell or tax simulation: honeypots and sell tax are not detected before the buy (only a buy dry-run is used). Buy tax is measured after the fill. Spend is bounded by maxSpend and maxAttempts.
- The test token has no tax, blacklist, max-wallet or max-tx rules; real launches do.
- Detection is Uniswap-V2-style pools against WETH only (no v3/v4, no launchpad curves). No mempool: the sniper always acts after the launch is mined, so it is never first.
- Trailing stops are sampled at tick time: a spike and fall between two ticks is not seen. Exit orders compete for nothing else here; no MEV, no competing bots.
- The reorg is simulated with evm_snapshot/evm_revert.
