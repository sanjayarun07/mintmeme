## Not covered
- Prices come from one pool's reserves read at tick time: no TWAP, no oracle, no cross-venue comparison; a flash-loan style one-block price spike inside this pool would trigger an order (the executable-output and deviation checks limit, not remove, that exposure).
- Orders and reservations are in memory: after a restart they must be re-placed (in-flight executions are re-adopted and idempotency keys prevent double execution).
- Trailing stops, market-cap triggers and curve (launchpad) venues are not exercised on a real chain; curve venues expose no spot price here and are reported as not evaluable.
- Local anvil: no real mempool competition, latency or reorgs. Platform fees are not collected on-chain.
