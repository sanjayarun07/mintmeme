## Not covered
- Only transactions SENT BY the leader EOA are seen: smart-contract wallets, aggregator-internal swaps and bundled/relayed txs are not.
- No mempool: the earliest signal is a mined leader block, so followers always trade after the leader (no front-running, and the leader's own price impact is already in the pool).
- Leader and follower are both ours on a private fork: no competing bots, no MEV, no real latency.
- Only WETH/USDC through Uniswap V2; tax, honeypot and rebasing tokens are not exercised here.
- The reorg is simulated with evm_snapshot/evm_revert and re-sending our own tx; a real reorg can also move or reorder our tx.
