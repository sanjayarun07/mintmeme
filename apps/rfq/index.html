    How an RFQ fill moves through the engine

    packages/sol/src/solchain.ts, tryRfq
    1. QuoteJupiter's /order is asked for JupiterZ only, for the wallet and exact amount. Our /build route is quoted alongside.Desk and engine
    2. DecideAbove the size threshold, no platform fee, and better than our route by the margin. Otherwise the normal route runs.Desk and engine
    3. GateTwo signers with the maker paying, one fill for the exact input, output at least the minimum to your own account, nothing else touching your wallet.Desk and engine
    4. SimulateAgainst mainnet without signatures: what the wallet spends and receives. The share-price check runs on the real output.Desk and engine
    5. Sign and executeThe Privy wallet signs slot 1. Jupiter's /execute adds the maker's signature and lands it. Retries resend identical bytes, so it lands at most once.Engine only

    The desk never signs or sends. Research, sources and limits: docs/JUPITERZ.md.
